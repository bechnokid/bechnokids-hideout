---
title: Some Thoughts on "GTA with Angry Cowboys"
date: 2026-09-13
---
In June, I started my own game of *Red Dead Redemption 2* on the PS5 after watching my wife's own endeavors while playing the game.

This will not be a detailed review since thousands of other writers conveyed the same feelings I have for the game much better than I ever could, but I just wanted to express my love for a game that I never thought I'd enjoy, especially not from the same company that made the *Grand Theft Auto* series.

I remember being unsure about playing it at first, assuming that its gameplay would require "high-tier gamer skills" after seeing how many things the player would need to keep track of. However, I decided to bite the bullet (heh), and play it because I wanted to experience it myself, newbie mistakes be damned.

Suffice to say, I was *hooked*, having consistently played every day. There were long stretches of time when I wouldn't update my website or draw, oops. {% emote 'embarrassed' %} Some would assume that I took a "hiatus", but in reality, I just got distracted playing a game about a cowboy who loves taking care of horses more than he likes dealing with people.

Having poured at least 200 hours into *RDR2* and also having started a second playthrough, I can say that it is one of, if not **the** best video game I have ever played. As much as I love *Monster Hunter Wilds*, I cannot deny the sheer amount of love and effort that was put into making *RDR2* from its beautiful environments, addicting gameplay, well-written and complex characters, and heart-shattering story that made me sob for 500 years.

I enjoyed the game on a surface level, but there are so many layers to it that hit me really hard but in a good way! Despite its initial impression on me "just another GTA game but you play as a cowboy", *RDR2* actually left me with a slightly more positive outlook on life, which is more than I could ever ask for in a video game. {% emote 'happy' %}

While I would recommend this game to everyone and their mother, I can definitely say it's not a game for someone who's deeply involved in fandoms. There aren't many conventionally attractive characters, nor is there any shipping potential between any of the characters unless you squint hard enough. Also, due to the absense of tropes and melodrama that most would see in anime and other media, the story can easily go over someone's head unless they're at least in their mid-20's.

This isn't to say that it isn't a popular game! It's one of the best-selling games of all time, after all! However, I think it says something when I barely see it being discussed in fandom spaces, at least compared to other media like The Amazing Digital Circus, Deltarune, etc.

It can be a bit lonely, but if I'll be honest: I'm content with just discussing the game with my wife, sharing funny stories from our own playthroughs, and repeating inside jokes such as, "finding the ice cream badger". I don't expect anyone to play this game, but if anyone reading this hasn't played this game, then I definitely recommend at least giving it a try and get to know the characters before writing it off as a game with just guns and violence!

With that, I end this short entry with my favorite quote from the game:

>Take a gamble that love exists, and do a loving act.
